Questions

Is TrialGuard free?

Yes. The free version tracks up to 5 subscriptions and trials with reminders, one-tap cancel links and the savings tracker. TrialGuard Pro unlocks unlimited items, widgets, the calendar, insights, Face ID lock and backup for $2.99/month, $14.99/year (with a 1-week free trial) or $29.99 once.

Do you need my bank login?

No, and we never will. TrialGuard doesn't connect to banks or read your email. You pick what you pay for, and everything stays on your iPhone.

How do reminders work?

TrialGuard schedules local notifications on your device before each trial ends or bill renews: by default the day before at 9:00 AM. With Pro you can be reminded up to a week ahead and on the day itself. No server is involved.

Can I cancel the Pro free trial?

Yes, anytime during the week, in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends and you won't be charged. (TrialGuard will happily remind you.)
