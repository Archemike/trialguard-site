FAQ

I'm not getting reminders.
Open iOS Settings › Notifications › TrialGuard and allow notifications. Reminders arrive at the time chosen in TrialGuard › Settings (9:00 AM by default).
Does TrialGuard cancel subscriptions for me?
No. It reminds you so you can cancel with the provider. For App Store subscriptions, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
How do I restore Pro on a new device?
Open TrialGuard › Settings › Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.
How do I cancel TrialGuard Pro?
Settings › your name › Subscriptions › TrialGuard. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.
Where is my data?
Only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
