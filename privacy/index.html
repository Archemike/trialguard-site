Privacy Policy

TrialGuard · Effective November 1, 2026

TrialGuard ("the app") is made by an independent developer. This policy explains how the app handles your information. The short version: we don't collect any.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and it does not send your information to us or to any third party.

Data stored on your device

The subscriptions, trials, notes, and settings you enter are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, protected by iOS data protection. Deleting the app deletes this data. If you use iCloud or Finder device backups, iOS may include this data in those backups, which Apple governs.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No server is involved.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Data export

If you use CSV export, the file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Open an issue at GitHub, or see the support page.
